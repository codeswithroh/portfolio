Pitch Video

Record yourself reading the pitch. Your agent does the edit.

Open live app Source

What it does

Pitch Video is an agent skill that takes one talking-head take and returns a launch-ready product demo. It trims dead air, films the product with Playwright, captions the result and scores it.

Worth knowing

  • Everything runs locally with ffmpeg, whisper.cpp and Playwright
  • Speeds speech up 1.1× without the chipmunk effect
  • Output is checked by measurement before it is handed back
